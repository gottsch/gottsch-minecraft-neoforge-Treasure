Treasure chests hold weapons you can't craft. The rarer the chest, the better the steel. Hover over a weapon to see its
damage and speed.

## Shapes {#shapes}

- **Short swords** and **rapiers** are light and quick.
- **Swords**, **falchions** and **machetes** strike a balance.
- **Broadswords** hit harder, but swing slower.
- **Maces** hit hardest of all, and swing slowest.
- **Broad axes** and **dwarven axes** chop wood as well as foes.

## Metals {#metals}

- **Copper** is soft and wears out quickly. A **chipped** blade is a little weaker still.
- **Iron** is as good as vanilla iron.
- **Steel** lasts three times as long as copper, and hits harder than iron.
- **Shadow** and **Skull** weapons are rare finds, tougher than diamond.
