Treasure sites are haunted. Be wary: carelessness could cost you dearly.

## Mimics {#mimics}

A mimic is a shapeshifter that disguises itself as an object, most often a treasure chest. Its skin can take on the look
and feel of almost anything. Disguised, it waits patiently for someone to come close.

A chest that turns out to be a mimic wakes when you open it, or when you break it. There is no telling one from the
real thing until then.

## Bound Souls {#bound_souls}

Some who hid treasure gave their lives to guard it, binding their souls to the place where it lies. They rise to fight
whoever comes near.
