---
title: Treasure2
---

Treasure2 adds to the thrill of discovery: rare chests of treasure, hidden all over the world. The chests are secured
by an assortment of locks, and only the right keys will open them.

This manual covers the chests, their locks and keys, the weapons you'll find inside, and the things that guard them.

> While you carry this manual, hover over a Treasure2 item in your inventory and press **B** to look it up.
