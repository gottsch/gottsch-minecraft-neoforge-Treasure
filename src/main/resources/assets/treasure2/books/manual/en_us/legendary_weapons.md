A handful of weapons are one of a kind. Each has a name, a story, and no equal. They turn up only in the rarest
loot, and they can't be repaired, so wield them wisely.

Most of them have a **power attack**: a chance, on each hit, to deal extra damage.

## Snake Eye's Katana {#snake_eyes_katana}

```spotlight treasure2:snake_eyes_katana
**Loot rarity:** Rare and Epic\
**Power attack:** 25% chance, +5 damage

> Move with the wind, and you will never be heard.
```
