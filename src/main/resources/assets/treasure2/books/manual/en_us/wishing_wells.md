Wishing wells and fountains are rare, and can appear almost anywhere in the Overworld. Look for a ring of flowers (or
cacti, in the desert) and an item frame holding a gold coin.

## Making a Wish {#wish}

Toss a coin or a gem into the well's water, and loot comes back out. The rarer the wish, the better the loot.

## Four-Leaf Clovers {#clover}

```spotlight treasure2:clover
Clovers sometimes grow around a wishing well. They can be picked, but not planted again.
```

Use a clover on something built from cobblestone, mossy cobblestone, stone bricks or mossy stone bricks to make a
wishing well of your own. It changes the blocks within 4 blocks of where you used it, as long as they touch each other.

## Locked Chests {#locked}

Can't find the keys? Toss the whole locked chest into a wishing well. **Every** lock comes off.

It costs you the well: its blocks turn back into plain stone. You have been warned!
