Treasure chests appear in the Overworld by default, though a server can let them appear in any dimension. A few sit on
the surface; most are buried or sunk underwater. Look for the markers above them: gravestones, skulls and crossbones, or
a ruin such as a tower or a crypt.

The rarer the chest, the better its loot, and the harder its locks. Locks must all come off before a chest will open.
See [Locks](entry:locks) and [Keys](entry:keys).

> Not every chest is a chest. See [Mimics](entry:mimics).

## Chests of Note {#notable}

### Wither Chest {#wither_chest}

Found only at the heart of a [Wither Grove](entry:wither_grove). It stands two blocks tall.

### Celestial and Infernal Chests {#mythical_chests}

The rarest chests of all, made for Mythical treasure: the kind that can hold the greatest [legendary weapons](entry:legendary_weapons).

### Compressor Chest {#compressor_chest}

Holds nearly twice as much as a normal chest.

### Bone Chest {#bone_chest}

Its lock is part of it and never comes off. A [Bone Key](entry:keys#bone_key) unlocks it, and the same key locks it again, so a Bone Chest is a safe place for your own loot.

### Skull Chests {#skull_chests}

Can be crafted, so you can keep your own loot in one.
