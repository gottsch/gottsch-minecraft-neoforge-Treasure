## Finding Treasure {#finding}

Treasure chests are hidden all over the world. Look for the signs above them:

- **Gravestones** and **skulls and crossbones** mark a chest buried below.
- **Ruins**, such as towers and crypts, keep a chest inside.
- **Wishing wells** hold loot of their own; see [Wishing Wells](entry:wishing_wells).
- **Wither trees** hide a Wither Chest beneath their roots; see [Wither Groves](entry:wither_grove).

Dig carefully. What sleeps beside a chest doesn't always stay asleep: see [Mimics and Bound Souls](entry:mimics).

## Opening a Chest {#opening}

1. Find out what's guarding it. Each [Lock](entry:locks) needs its own [Key](entry:keys).
2. Hold the key and **sneak + use** it on the chest. The lock comes off, and the key loses a use.
3. When every lock is off, open the chest.

No key? A [lock pick](entry:lock_picks) might do, or a [wishing well](entry:wishing_wells#locked) will take the locks
off for a price.

## Rarity {#rarity}

Chests, locks, keys and loot all have a rarity. From most to least common:

1. Common
2. Uncommon
3. Scarce
4. Rare
5. Epic
6. Legendary
7. Mythical
