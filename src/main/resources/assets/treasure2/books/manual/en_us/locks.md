Locks keep treasure chests shut. Every lock has a [Key](entry:keys) of its own, and some can also be opened by special
keys or [lock picks](entry:lock_picks). Hover over a lock in your inventory to see which.

Locks belong to a **category**: Elemental, Metal, Gems, Mob or Wither. Some special keys open every lock of one
category.

A lock taken off a chest keeps working. Put locks on your own treasure chests to protect your loot.
