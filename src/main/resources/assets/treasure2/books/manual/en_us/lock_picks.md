Lock picks are cheap to craft, and open any low-rarity lock, but only some of the time. A failed pick can break.
Use them like a key: **sneak + use** on the chest.

The chances below are the defaults. A server can change them; the pick's tooltip always shows the real ones.

## Pilferer's Lock Pick {#pilferers_lock_pick}

```spotlight treasure2:pilferers_lock_pick
The cheapest pick, with the lowest chance. Meant for the early game.

**Common locks:** 48%\
**Uncommon locks:** 32%\
**Uses:** 10
```

## Thief's Lock Pick {#thiefs_lock_pick}

```spotlight treasure2:thiefs_lock_pick
Costs more than a Pilferer's pick, but succeeds more often, and can work on Scarce locks too.

**Common locks:** 60%\
**Uncommon locks:** 45%\
**Scarce locks:** 30%\
**Uses:** 10
```
