Somewhere in the Overworld, the land turns grey around a great dead tree. This is a wither grove, and a
Wither Chest lies beneath its roots.

## The Wither Tree {#tree}

At the grove's heart stands the main wither tree. Below it, a pit leads down through the dark to a treasure room, and
a [Wither Chest](entry:chests#wither_chest) sealed with a [Wither Lock](entry:locks#list).

Only a [Wither Key](entry:keys#wither_key) or the One Key opens it. A Skeleton Key won't.

Its loot is about as good as a Scarce chest's, with more potions.

## The Mist {#mist}

The grove's roots and moss breathe out a deadly mist:

- **Wither roots** give off a mist that **withers** whoever stands near.
- **Spanish moss** drips a mist that **poisons** whoever stands beneath it.

Torches drive the mist back. A couple of torches near a root thin it out; enough of them stop it.

## Guardians {#guardians}

The grove has its own monsters: wither skeletons, strays, skeletons, spiders, and
[Witherwood Golems](entry:mimics#witherwood_golem) that roam among the trees.
